This course introduces the generation of synthetic data with deep generative models. We cover why synthetic data is useful, how two families of models produce it, and how to judge whether the result is any good.

## Who this course is for

The course assumes basic Python and an introductory course in machine learning. You do not need prior experience with generative models.
