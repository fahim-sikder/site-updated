The course runs over 10 consecutive weeks, followed by two weeks for the reflection report: 12 weeks in total. It has 13 sessions of 2 hours each: 6 lectures, 1 guest lecture, 3 labs, 2 paper seminars and 1 project presentation seminar.

- **Weeks 1 to 3, 5, 6, 9 and 10:** one session per week.
- **Weeks 4, 7 and 8:** two sessions per week, a lecture or seminar followed by a lab.

> [!NOTE]
> Dates, times and rooms are to be announced.
