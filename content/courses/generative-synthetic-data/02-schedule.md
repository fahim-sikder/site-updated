The course runs over 10 consecutive weeks, followed by two weeks for the reflection report: 12 weeks in total. Every session lasts 2 hours.

- **Weeks 1 to 3 and 8 to 10:** one session per week.
- **Weeks 4 to 7:** two sessions per week, a lecture or seminar followed by a lab.

> [!NOTE]
> Dates, times and rooms are to be announced.
