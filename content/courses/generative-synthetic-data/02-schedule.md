Classes take place every two weeks. The course has 13 sessions of 2 hours each: 6 lectures, 1 guest lecture, 3 labs, 2 paper seminars and 1 project presentation seminar. In three of the classes, a lecture or seminar is followed by a lab. The reflection report is due two weeks after the final presentations.

> [!NOTE]
> Dates, times and rooms are to be announced.
