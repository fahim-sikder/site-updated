This elective PhD course teaches you to train generative models for synthetic data and to evaluate the result for utility, privacy and fairness. Over 10 weeks it combines lectures, hands-on labs, paper seminars and an individual project on data from your own research field.

> [!NOTE] Next course run
> The term and the weekday of the sessions are to be announced. Contact the course leader if you would like to take the course.

## At a glance

- **Credits:** 6 hp (6 ECTS), graded pass/fail
- **Level:** PhD, elective
- **Length:** 10 weeks of sessions, then 2 weeks to write the reflection report
- **Sessions:** 14 sessions of 2 hours (28 contact hours): 6 lectures, 1 guest lecture, 4 labs, 2 paper seminars and 1 project session
- **Department:** IDA (Department of Computer and Information Science), Linköping University
- **Examiner and lecturer:** Md Fahim Sikder
- **Computing:** a laptop that runs Docker. No GPU is needed.
