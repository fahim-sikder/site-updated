## Computing resources

The labs use small public datasets, so they run on a laptop CPU or a free cloud server. No GPU access is required. You need a personal laptop that can run Docker; the [Labs](../04-labs/) page explains the set-up.

## Sensitive data

You may use your own data in the project only if your ethical approvals and data agreements allow it. Do not share any real sensitive data within the course. In the [project](../06-project/), a public dataset with a similar structure can replace your real data.

## Sustainability

You log the energy use of your training runs with [CodeCarbon](https://github.com/mlco2/codecarbon), in the labs and in the project.

## Online and hybrid participation

The course can be given online or in hybrid form if there are participants from outside Linköping. Contact the course leader if you would like to take part remotely.

## Course evaluation

You give feedback at mid-course and at the end of the course. The course leader uses this feedback to revise the next course run.
