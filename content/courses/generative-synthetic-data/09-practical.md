## Computing resources

The [Labs](../04-labs/#set-up) page explains what participants need for the labs.

## Sensitive data

Participants may use their own data in the project only if their ethical approvals and data agreements allow it, and must not share any real sensitive data within the course. In the [project](../06-project/), a public dataset with a similar structure can replace the real data.

## Sustainability

Participants log the energy use of their training runs with [CodeCarbon](https://github.com/mlco2/codecarbon), in the labs and in the project.

## Online and hybrid participation

The course can be given online or in hybrid form if there are participants from outside Linköping. Participants who would like to take part remotely should contact the examiner.

## Course evaluation

Participants give feedback at mid-course and at the end of the course. The examiner uses this feedback to revise the next course run.
