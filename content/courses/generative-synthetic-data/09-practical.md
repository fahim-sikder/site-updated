## Computing resources

The labs use small public datasets, so they run on a laptop CPU or a free cloud server. No GPU access is required. You need a personal laptop that can run Docker; the [Labs](../04-labs/) page explains the set-up.

## Sensitive data

Follow your own ethical approvals and data agreements. Participants share no real sensitive data within the course. In the [project](../06-project/), a public dataset with a similar structure can replace your real data.

## Sustainability

You log the energy use of your training runs with CodeCarbon, in the labs and in the project.

## Course evaluation

You give feedback at mid-course and at the end of the course. The course leader uses this feedback to revise the next course run.
