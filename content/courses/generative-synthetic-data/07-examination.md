The course is graded pass/fail and gives 8 hp (8 ECTS). To pass, you must complete all five parts below.

| Part | What is required |
|---|---|
| Lectures and seminars | Attendance and active participation in the [lectures](../03-lectures/) and [paper seminars](../05-paper-seminars/). |
| Labs | Completion of all three [lab sessions](../04-labs/). |
| Paper seminar | Leading the discussion of one paper, in one of the two [paper seminars](../05-paper-seminars/). |
| Individual project | Develop a generative model (new, or taken from the literature), train it on data from your own domain, and evaluate the synthetic data for privacy, utility and fairness (if applicable). You present the project in the final session. See the [project page](../06-project/). |
| Reflection report | A written report, following the [given instructions](../06-project/#reflection-report), due four weeks after the final presentations. It documents the project findings and reflects on how your assumptions and practice around synthetic data have changed. |

**Examiner:** Md Fahim Sikder
