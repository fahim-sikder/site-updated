The course is graded pass/fail and gives 8 hp (8 ECTS). To pass, a participant must complete all five parts below.

| Part | What is required |
|---|---|
| Lectures and seminars | Attendance and active participation in the [lectures](../03-lectures/) and [paper seminars](../05-paper-seminars/). Participants must attend at least 80% of the lectures. |
| Labs | Completion of all three [lab sessions](../04-labs/). |
| Paper seminar | Leading the discussion of one paper, in one of the two [paper seminars](../05-paper-seminars/). |
| Individual project | Develop a generative model (new, or taken from the literature), train it on data from the participant's own domain, and evaluate the synthetic data for privacy, utility and fairness (if applicable). The project is presented in the final session. See the [project page](../06-project/). |
| Reflection report | A written report, following the [given instructions](../06-project/#reflection-report), due four weeks after the final presentations. It documents the project findings and reflects on how the participant's assumptions and practice around synthetic data have changed. |
