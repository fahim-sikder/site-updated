Generative Models for Synthetic Data

Generative Models for Synthetic Data: Privacy, Fairness and Utility

This new PhD course teaches participants to train generative models for synthetic data and to evaluate the result for utility, privacy and fairness. It combines lectures, hands-on labs, paper seminars and an individual project on data from each participant’s own research field.

At a glance

  • Credits: 8 hp (8 ECTS), graded pass/fail
  • Level: PhD
  • Sessions: 13 sessions of 2 hours (26 contact hours): 6 lectures, 1 guest lecture, 3 labs, 2 paper seminars and 1 project presentation seminar
  • Department: IDA (Department of Computer and Information Science), Linköping University
  • Examiner and lecturer: Md Fahim Sikder
  • Computing: a laptop that runs Docker. No GPU is needed.
  • Format: on campus, or online or hybrid if there are participants from outside Linköping

Registration

  • IDA PhD students: register through the IDA Graduate Portal, by the deadline the department sends out by email.
  • PhD students from outside IDA or LiU: email md.fahim.sikder@liu.se to express your interest, no later than 31 December 2026. The examiner will then send you instructions on how to register.
